Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026 100% Secured Shopping

At AuraStore, we prioritize the privacy and security of our storefront visitors. Because our store runs as a 100% Free, direct checkout engine in your browser, we do not require user account registrations, passwords, or credit card verification details on our servers.

1. Direct Browser-to-WhatsApp Checkout

Our ordering system is fully client-side. When you build a cart and initiate order checkout, the details are compiled locally in your browser and formatted into a structured text receipt. This receipt is directly transmitted to our store's official WhatsApp business contact number via WhatsApp web/app link protocols. We do not store or process your order data, name, physical address, or phone number on any external server.

2. Information We Process

To fulfill your direct orders, the storefront processes the following shipping information locally on your device:

  • Recipient Full Name (For sorting and identification on package arrivals).
  • Delivery Address (To arrange shipping and transport).
  • Phone Number (To contact you directly regarding delivery issues).
  • Additional Order Notes (Any special customization requests).

This information is only saved in your browser's local cache (localStorage) for autofill convenience during future visits.

3. Data Storage (Local Storage)

Your wishlist items, storefront theme configurations (Dark/Light mode), and checkout form data are saved locally on your device using HTML5 Web Storage (localStorage). You can clear this data at any time by clearing your web browser cookies and site cache.

4. Third-Party Integrations & Hosting

Because AuraStore runs completely client-side, we do not host backend databases. The platform utilizes standard, trusted CDNs (like Google Fonts and FontAwesome) to render user interface icons and typography. When you click checkout, the site opens the official WhatsApp API portal to start a chat with our representative.

5. Contacting the Webmaster

If you have any questions or concerns regarding shopping privacy, please feel free to reach out to us directly through our storefront contact details page or drop us an email.
